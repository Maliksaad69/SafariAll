# Sources for park and country guide data

Park facts in `init/01-safaribookings.sql` (areas, founding years, UNESCO/Ramsar
status, wildlife counts and access) were checked against these Wikipedia
articles in October 2026. Figures such as animal counts are the most recent ones
those articles cite and will change over time.

- Tanzania: [Tarangire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarangire_National_Park), [Lake Manyara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Manyara_National_Park), [Nyerere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyerere_National_Park), [Ruaha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruaha_National_Park), [Gombe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gombe_Stream_National_Park), [Katavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katavi_National_Park), [Zanzibar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanzibar)
- Kenya: [Amboseli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amboseli_National_Park), [Lake Nakuru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Nakuru_National_Park), [Samburu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samburu_National_Reserve), [Buffalo Springs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buffalo_Springs_National_Reserve), [Tsavo East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsavo_East_National_Park)
- Botswana: [Chobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chobe_National_Park), [Okavango Delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Okavango_Delta), [Moremi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moremi_Game_Reserve), [Central Kalahari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Kalahari_Game_Reserve), [Makgadikgadi Pan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Makgadikgadi_Pan), [Nxai Pan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nxai_Pan_National_Park)
- South Africa: [Kruger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kruger_National_Park), [Sabi Sand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabi_Sand_Game_Reserve), [MalaMala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mala_Mala_Game_Reserve), [Phinda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phinda_Private_Game_Reserve), [Hluhluwe-iMfolozi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hluhluwe%E2%80%93Imfolozi_Park), [Addo Elephant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addo_Elephant_National_Park)
- Uganda: [Bwindi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bwindi_Impenetrable_National_Park), [Queen Elizabeth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_National_Park), [Murchison Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murchison_Falls_National_Park)
- Zambia: [South Luangwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Luangwa_National_Park), [Lower Zambezi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_Zambezi_National_Park)
- Namibia: [Etosha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etosha_National_Park), [Sossusvlei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sossusvlei)
- Zimbabwe: [Hwange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwange_National_Park), [Mana Pools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mana_Pools_National_Park), [Victoria Falls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Falls)
- Rwanda: [Volcanoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volcanoes_National_Park), [Akagera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akagera_National_Park)
